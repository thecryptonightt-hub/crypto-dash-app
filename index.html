5-question framework check — on top of the 11 factors

Every token is also checked against 5 plain questions, built from the same 11-factor evidence — nothing is removed, and the factors this framework does not cover (adoption, competitive advantage, security and team, risk) stay in the score. Tap any token for its framework check.

1. Real revenue? Real fees from real users, over at least 30 days.  2. Does revenue reach the token? Buybacks, burns, or fee distributions.  3. Real utility and flywheel? Holding it gives real exposure and value grows with use.  4. Supply unlocks? Emissions, unlock dates, and fully diluted value versus market cap.  5. A dated catalyst? A launch, partnership, listing, license, or integration with an actual date. Dated catalysts stay UNKNOWN when the free feeds do not carry them — never guessed.

Winners usually fall into two buckets: cash-flow projects you can value like a company, and game-changing technology that solves a problem nothing else solves.
